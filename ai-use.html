Tools and models

  • OpenAI Codex desktop coding agent. The exact underlying model label was not displayed in this session, so I do not invent a version number.
  • VALORANT-API release-13.10 was the data source. It is a community-maintained API derived from game data, not a generative model and not an official Riot Games API.
  • No AI-generated translations were inserted into the corpus. The bilingual strings come from the API records and retain their UUID provenance.

Division of work

AI did

  • Tested whether different FPS data sources could provide enough aligned English–Simplified Chinese entries.
  • Wrote the repeatable Python collection script, aligned records by UUID, removed exact duplicates, retained all eligible pairs, and created the fixed 50-item audit sample.
  • Built the first-pass strategy classifications from the codebook, the static bilingual website, the linked D3 visualisations and the first drafts of the report and codebook.
  • Checked the site's data counts, chart-table consistency and URL state during local testing.

I did

  • I chose VALORANT and the English → Simplified Chinese direction.
  • I reviewed the 50-item audit sample against the source records. All 50 are now marked human_checked=yes; the rest of the corpus remains uncleared.
  • I am responsible for rewording the codebook definitions in my own words before the oral presentation and for explaining one boundary case.
  • I must check the final GitHub repository, site URL and any institutional or platform terms before submission.

Example prompts that worked well

“I want to work on VALORANT in English and Simplified Chinese. Can you crawl enough bilingual entries, and what data source is actually reproducible?”

This prompt worked because it specified the game, languages and practical constraint. It led to a source comparison rather than a generic topic list.

“Build a full eligible bilingual corpus and a static site with search, category and human-check filters, at least three genuinely linked visualisations, a codebook and a learning report.”

This prompt worked because the deliverables were concrete and testable. It made the requirements explicit while allowing the corpus to grow beyond the 500-entry minimum.

One thing AI got wrong

The first plan was to use English and Simplified Chinese FPS store-page descriptions from Steam. The AI initially treated that as a simple bilingual corpus. A pilot extraction of 15 popular FPS pages produced only around 245 roughly aligned sentence units, and the Chinese pages often merged or split sentences differently. The plan was therefore not a reliable route to 500 sentence pairs without mixing many unrelated games and text types.

The problem was noticed by checking the actual counts and alignment rather than trusting the apparent availability of the pages. The project switched to VALORANT spray names, a coherent item type with stable UUID alignment and 939 eligible pairs. This is a useful example of AI's tendency to make a plausible workflow sound easier than it is until the data are tested.

Responsibility and limits

AI was useful for scale, consistency and implementation, but it cannot replace the author's judgement about whether a creative equivalence works, whether a community reference is being read correctly, or whether a category boundary is defensible. The audit sample is deliberately public so that the human verification step is visible rather than hidden behind a claim of perfect data.

The site publishes only short names and source links. It does not reproduce complete game assets, documents or personal data. The source remains a third-party community API; the API version and retrieval date are stated on the explorer page.

有效的提示例子

「我想做 VALORANT，英文和简体中文。你能爬够双语条目吗？哪个数据来源真正可重现？」

这个提示有效，因为它指明游戏、语言与实际限制，促使 AI 比较数据来源，而不是只给泛泛题目。

「建立全量合格双语语料和静态网站，包含搜索、类别与人工核查筛选、至少三个真正联动的可视化、标注手册和学习报告。」

这个提示有效，因为交付物具体且可测试，同时允许语料规模超过 500 条最低要求。

AI 犯过的一个错误

最初计划是使用 Steam 上 FPS 游戏的英文与简中商店页描述。AI 起初把它视为简单的双语语料库。但试验性抽取 15 款热门 FPS 页面后，只得到约 245 个粗略对齐的句子单位，而且中文页面经常合并或拆分句子。若不混合大量无关游戏与文本类型，这并不是稳定取得 500 对句子的方案。

问题是在检查实际数量与对齐质量后发现的，而不是只相信页面表面上有很多文字。项目因此改用 VALORANT 喷漆名称：类型一致、UUID 对齐稳定，并有 939 个合格配对。这也说明 AI 容易把看似可行的流程说得比实际简单，直到数据被真正测试。

责任与限制

AI 在规模、一致性与实作方面有帮助，但不能取代作者判断：某个创意对应是否成立、某个社群引用是否理解正确、某个类别边界是否站得住脚。抽查样本刻意公开，让人工核查步骤可见，而不是隐藏在「数据完美」的宣称背后。

网站只发布短名称与来源链接，不重制完整游戏资产、文件或个人数据。来源为第三方社群 API；探勘页已列明 API 版本与采集日期。
